COMPANY LEAVE POLICY
====================

Annual Leave: 20 days per year.
Sick Leave: 10 days per year. Doctor's note needed after 2 days.
Maternity Leave: 26 weeks paid.
Paternity Leave: 4 weeks paid.
Public Holidays: 12 days. Double pay if you work on them.


Unpaid Leave: Up to 12 weeks, manager approval needed.
